Privacy policy · Last updated October 19, 2026

Aurelle doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings and Daily results are saved on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, they also sync between your own devices through Apple's iCloud. I can't see that data.

Game Center

If you use Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center under your Game Center profile, as described in Apple's privacy policy.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. I don't receive your payment details.

Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled on your device and only if you turn them on.

Children

Aurelle is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
