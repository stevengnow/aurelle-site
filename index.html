AURELLE

A memory puzzle for iPhone and iPad.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: hello@aetherline.app. I read everything.

Purchases

Bought something on another device? Open the Shop (the bag on the home screen) and tap Restore purchases. Purchases follow your Apple Account.

Progress and iCloud

Your progress syncs through iCloud when you're signed in on each device. If it isn't showing up, check that iCloud is on for Aurelle in the Settings app.

Daily reminders

Turn them on in Aurelle's Settings. If they don't arrive, check that notifications are allowed for Aurelle in the Settings app.
